The Mediterranean

For summers you'll wish could last forever.

There is a certain kind of magic to a Mediterranean summer — slow mornings by the sea, hidden coves reached by boat, long lunches that somehow turn into dinner and evenings with nowhere else to be.

From the timeless beauty of the Italian coast and the effortless energy of the Greek islands to the shores of the South of France and Bodrum, these are places we return to again and again. We seek out the hotels with a sense of place, the tables worth planning around and those special corners you'll want to keep to yourself.

Italy · Greece · France · Spain · Turkey

The Far East

For going further — and discovering something entirely new.

Some journeys are about switching off. Others are about feeling completely awake.

The Far East is a world of contrasts — the energy of Tokyo, the calm of a secluded island, centuries-old traditions alongside extraordinary contemporary design, and some of the most exciting food you'll find anywhere.

Whether you're discovering Japan for the first time, travelling through Vietnam or escaping to the beaches of Thailand and Indonesia, these are journeys made for curiosity — with beautiful places to stay along the way.

Japan · Vietnam · Thailand · Indonesia

The Wild

For the kind of journey that stays with you.

There are places you visit, and then there are places you feel.

Days here begin differently — early mornings in the bush, landscapes that seem to go on forever and moments in the wild that no photograph quite manages to capture. And at the end of it all, some of the world's most beautiful lodges waiting for you.

From South Africa to Kenya, Tanzania and Botswana, we create journeys that bring together adventure, exceptional stays and experiences that feel genuinely once in a lifetime.

South Africa · Kenya · Tanzania · Botswana
