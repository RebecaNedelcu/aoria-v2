## Homepage video

`../videos/hero-coast.mp4` and `../videos/hero-coast-mobile.mp4` are local edits of [Drone Shot of a Beautiful Calm Beach Shore](https://www.pexels.com/video/drone-shot-of-a-beautiful-calm-beach-shore-4188463/) by **Mo Eid**, used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/).

The 4K original was trimmed from 00:02 to 00:12, with a one-second blend joining the end to the beginning for a nine-second loop. Both exports are silent H.264 MP4 at 24 fps with fast-start metadata. The desktop export is 1920 × 1080; the mobile export is a dedicated 720 × 1280 crop. The WebP poster images use each export's first frame. Only the appropriate video is requested, and reduced-motion, data-saving, and very slow connections default to the poster until the visitor presses play.
