Who we are

AORIA S.R.L. is responsible for this website. For questions about privacy or to exercise a data protection right, email hello@aoria-travel.com or call +40 745 621 200.

Information and purposes

When you browse

The server delivering this website receives technical request information, such as your IP address, the page requested, the time of the request and browser details. This is needed to display the site and help keep it secure. Where AORIA processes this information, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating and protecting the website under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR.

When you use the enquiry form

The current enquiry form is a visual preview. You can enter a name, number and ages of travellers, travel dates, destinations and preferences, but submitting it does not send those details to AORIA or store them in this website. The privacy checkbox is part of that preview; checking it does not transmit your details. You do not need to fill in the form to browse the website, and the site does not make automated decisions about visitors.

Before the form is connected to a live enquiry service, we will update this policy with the processing purpose, legal basis, recipients, any international transfers and a retention period for enquiries.

Fonts, cookies and analytics

This website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. To display them, your browser makes a request to Google, which may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details. You can read Google’s privacy policy for information about its handling of data.

The website code does not currently set cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, we will update this notice and provide any choices required by law before using them.

Sharing and international transfers

We do not receive or share the details entered in the current enquiry form. The provider that serves the website may handle technical request data, and Google receives requests when its fonts are loaded. The hosting provider has not yet been confirmed for the live website, so its identity and any applicable transfer arrangements will be added here before launch.

How long information is kept

AORIA does not retain information entered into the preview enquiry form. The final hosting service may keep technical access or security logs; its retention period has not yet been confirmed and will be added to this notice when hosting is selected. Google handles its own font-service requests under its privacy information.

Your rights

Where AORIA processes your personal data, you may ask to access it, correct it, delete it or restrict its use. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Data portability and the right to withdraw consent apply where relevant. These rights have conditions and exceptions under the GDPR.

To make a request, email hello@aoria-travel.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority in the country where you live, work or where you believe a breach occurred. The European Data Protection Board lists national authorities.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the website’s data practices change, including when enquiries become live or a hosting provider is confirmed.
